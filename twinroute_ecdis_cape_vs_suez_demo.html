What am I looking at?

The real-world scenario
Since late 2023, most container carriers — including Maersk — have diverted from the Suez Canal around the Cape of Good Hope to avoid attacks on shipping in the Red Sea. As of 2026 this diversion is still largely in effect. This demo models that exact decision.
Static routing
One route is planned before departure and never changes, even if new risk information arrives mid-voyage.
Adaptive routing (TADIF)
The proposed system: a "digital twin" keeps re-checking conditions during the voyage and recomputes the remaining route when something important changes.
Φtrigger (replan trigger)
The rule deciding *when* to recompute the route — here, when assessed security risk for the Red Sea corridor crosses a threshold.
Twin Confidence Index
A 0–1 score for how much the system trusts its own current picture of reality. It dips during uncertain moments like a replan.
Waypoint markers
The small squares/circles along each route, spaced at regular distance intervals — the same convention real ECDIS and voyage-planning software use.
